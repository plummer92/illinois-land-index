Your tax bill

See which districts receive your property taxes

Your bill divides the tax among the districts serving your parcel. Chatham’s village government receives only its listed share; schools, the county and other districts have their own shares.

Use your parcel’s county tax record for the actual amounts. The Treasurer identifies the current cycle as 2025 taxes payable in 2026. A district’s amount on your bill shows who receives the tax, not which purchase it paid for.

Budgeted internal allocation

Why did utility administration rise 46%?

$555,848 → $812,407

The administration allocation increased 46.16% from the FY2026 final appropriation to the FY2027 appropriation approved April 28, 2026. This is an allocation between village funds. It is not a 46% increase in a household utility rate, and an appropriation does not establish an actual payment.

Still needed: The current cost-allocation worksheet showing which central services utilities receive, how their share was calculated and what changed between years.

Financing approved March 24, 2026

New village hall: up to $17 million in financing

Chatham approved Bank of Springfield financing at 4.24% fixed for seven years. The terms provide two years of interest-only payments, followed by payments calculated over 25 years, leaving a balance due at maturity. Repayment comes from village general funds.

$17 million is the approved financing limit. It is not the current loan balance or final project cost. The audit separately reports a $9 million note issued May 21, 2026. These records do not establish $26 million in outstanding debt; the note, authorization and current draws still need to be reconciled.

Still needed: Current loan draws and balances, the full project budget, and the calculation of any utility share of building costs.

Read the financing and approval records
  • Ordinance 26-08 and financing terms — PDF pages 2–4; limit, lender, rate and repayment terms.
  • March 24, 2026 board minutes — PDF page 79 of the April 14 packet; five yes votes and one abstention.
  • FY2026 audit, subsequent events — PDF page 91; later $9 million note.

The posted financing ordinance has blank signature fields. The meeting minutes establish approval; they do not establish the amount drawn.

Posted order dated March 21, 2024

Billing software: cost and what customers receive

$42,900 a year + $15,000 migration

The posted Springbrook order lists a three-year term, annual increases capped at 7%, and automatic renewal unless either party gives at least 60 days’ notice before the term ends.

At the May 13, 2025 meeting, staff said the upgrade lacked budget-billing functionality and announced that the program would end with July billing. These records do not establish whether it was later restored.

Still needed: The signed order, current renewal invoice and migration credits. The posted signature page is blank, so the quoted price is not a verified current invoice.
