Disclaimer & Privacy

Illinois Land Index is built to help people organize and understand public parcel information. It is a research tool, not a substitute for professional due diligence.

No Professional Advice

Illinois Land Index does not provide legal, tax, title, appraisal, surveying, engineering, zoning, environmental, financial, or investment advice. Any scores, estimates, filters, or summaries are for research and prioritization only.

Verify Public Records

Parcel data can be incomplete, delayed, outdated, or interpreted incorrectly. Before buying, selling, contacting an owner, or making any land-related decision, verify records directly with the appropriate county offices, recorder, treasurer, assessor, GIS department, zoning authority, and qualified professionals.

Estimated Values

Any estimated market values are rough proxies based on available assessment data. They are not appraisals. Farmland, exempt property, special-use property, and recently improved property may not be represented well by simple assessment-based estimates.

Public Records And Owner Data

Address suggestions on the homepage and parcel dashboard send the partial address you type to Sangamon County GIS after a short pause. Submitting a search sends the address or parcel number to the county GIS for lookup. These search details are not sent to analytics. Results link to county parcel and tax records. The county signals page publishes aggregate counts.

Map and parcel searches

The water map remembers the selected place and view in your browser. Opening a parcel on the water map passes a point inside its boundary so the map can select it.

Earlier form submissions

We do not sell contact details or research preferences submitted through the former interest form.

Visitor counting

We use Google Analytics to count page visits, navigation and successful property searches. A successful-search event records that a property was found, without the address, search text, parcel number, owner name or map coordinates. Our tracking removes query strings, map fragments and link text from its event details. Automatic enhanced measurement is disabled.

Sharing a property sends its public parcel link to the app or person you choose. The link contains a parcel number, without the address you typed. Link previews use a general Illinois Land Index image.

You can exclude this browser from visitor counting. The preference is saved in your browser and must be set separately on other devices. It does not change earlier reports.

Optional research support

The support page links to Ko-fi for optional one-time or monthly contributions. Ko-fi and its payment providers handle checkout under their own privacy policies. We do not collect payment details on Illinois Land Index or add your property search, parcel number or address to the payment link. A contribution is not required to use the property lookup or read the research, and does not affect parcel scores or research conclusions.
