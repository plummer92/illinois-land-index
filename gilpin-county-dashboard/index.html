Gilpin research stack

County-specific checks before pursuing land.

Terrain and access

Measure slope, usable building area, winter access, driveway grade, and road maintenance responsibility.

Wildfire exposure

Review defensible-space requirements, insurance availability, evacuation routes, and local fire-district coverage.

Water and sanitation

Confirm legal water, well feasibility, septic suitability, setbacks, and any municipal service boundary.

Mining and title

Check patented mining claims, mineral rights, mine features, easements, surveys, and title exceptions.

Taxes and value

Verify current tax status, assessed value, sale history, and special districts through the county treasurer and assessor.

Planning and zoning

Confirm use permissions, minimum lot size, building standards, short-term-rental rules, and permit history.

Public records are screening signals, not a title, survey, appraisal, or buildability determination. Verify every parcel with Gilpin County and qualified professionals.
