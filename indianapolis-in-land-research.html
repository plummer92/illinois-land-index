What the source provides

IndianaMap provides public parcel polygons, county identifiers, DLGF property class codes, township context, and source-agency provenance. The dashboard publishes aggregate counts and a small privacy-conscious geometry sample.

What the source does not provide

The current statewide layer does not include a usable acreage, assessed-value, market-value, or building-count field. The dashboard says those measures are unavailable. Candidate acreage is calculated from geometry and remains a screening estimate that needs county verification.

How to use the market page

Start with county coverage, inspect large-parcel samples, then move to the relevant county assessor and planning records. The dashboard is not a listing feed, valuation, survey, title report, or buildability determination.
