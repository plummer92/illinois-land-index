What this first release does not claim

The current statewide layer does not publish acreage, assessed value, market value, or building counts. Aggregate acreage and valuation statistics are marked unavailable. Candidate acreage is geometry-derived and intended only to shorten a research list.

Owner names, addresses, parcel identifiers, and mailing information are not included in these processed public files.
