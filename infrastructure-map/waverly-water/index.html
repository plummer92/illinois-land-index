Morgan & Sangamon counties / November 12, 2026

Would CyrusOne fit within Apple Creek’s water limit?

If Apple Creek supplied all four buildings, CyrusOne projects it would need about 140,400 gallons a month in routine operation. That demand would have fit in each historical year examined. Recent meter readings are still needed to tell how much room there is today.

How much of the allowance would be used?

The City of Waverly supplies Apple Creek Water Cooperative. Their contract allows 3 million gallons per month averaged over a year, or 36 million annually, for all co-op customers combined. Each row adds the same projected CyrusOne demand to a different year of Apple Creek purchases.

The number to watch is 2.86 million gallons a month.

That’s how much Apple Creek’s existing customers could average before CyrusOne’s projected use would bring the co-op to its limit. The four-building forecast is 4,616 gallons per day, or 1,684,840 per 365-day year. Subtract that from 36 million and the existing customers have about 2,859,600 gallons per average month.

Waverly’s contract calls for monthly itemized statements showing gallons delivered. The latest 12 statements would give residents a current average and show how high purchases climb in summer. Read the contract clauses (PDF).

Where the figures come from

2012 · Reported purchases
The Illinois State Water Survey records 20,068,000 gallons bought from Waverly and zero gallons from Apple Creek’s own sources. Dividing by 12 gives the monthly average. State purchase record.
2020 and 2022 · Estimated purchases
Apple Creek reported $84,586 in water purchases in 2020, when Waverly charged $3.40 per 1,000 gallons. In 2022 it reported $85,381 at $4.25 per 1,000 gallons. Dividing expenses by those rates gives about 24.88 million and 20.09 million gallons for the respective years. The 2022 return used cash accounting and was filed before its audit was finished, so payment dates can differ from delivery dates. 2020 return, Part IX · 2022 return, Part IX · 2022 audit note · Wholesale rate schedule (PDF).
Full campus · Projected demand
CyrusOne’s revised application projects average use of 4,616 gallons per day and a peak day of 11,504 gallons for all four buildings. The historical comparisons use the daily average and assume Apple Creek supplies all of it. The application describes one-time cooling-system and fire-tank fills separately. County zoning filing, page 10 · See the proposed campus on the map.
Summer peaks and drought
A single summer month above 3 million gallons can still fall within the annual average. Delivering enough water on a peak day is a separate question. During an extended shortage, the contract allows Waverly to reduce Apple Creek’s supply in the same proportion as it reduces supply to its own customers. Illinois EPA also requires purchased-water systems to record incoming gallons daily. Shortage provision (PDF) · Illinois EPA operating reports.
