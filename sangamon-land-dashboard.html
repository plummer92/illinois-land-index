How to use the Sangamon land dashboard

Use the parcel number from a tax bill or enter a street address followed by a comma and the city. For an address match, check the boundary in the county parcel viewer. Then use the county tax record to verify the assessment or payment history.

Who this is for

The dashboard is built for land buyers, local investors, brokers, researchers, and anyone trying to understand where overlooked acreage might exist around Chatham, Sangamon County, and Central Illinois growth corridors.
