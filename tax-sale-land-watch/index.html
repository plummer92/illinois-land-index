Illinois

Tax lien certificate

The annual sale does not convey immediate land ownership. Bidders compete by bidding the penalty rate down from a 9% ceiling. For redemption periods beginning after July 10, 2026, the ordinary period is three years, with a one-year period for certain vacant non-farm and other qualifying property. A tax deed requires a later court petition and strict notice compliance. In practice, plan for valuable property to redeem rather than assuming a deed.

35 ILCS 200/21-215 · 35 ILCS 200/21-350 · 35 ILCS 200/22-30

Indiana

Tax-sale certificate and lien

The ordinary county sale conveys a certificate and lien, not immediate title. The standard redemption period is generally one year; some county-acquired or commissioner-certificate routes can use 120 days. After redemption expires and required notices are complete, the certificate holder may petition for a tax deed.

Tennessee

Redeemable tax deed

Delinquent-tax property is sold under a court decree and remains subject to redemption after confirmation. For sales confirmed on or after July 1, 2026, the period is generally one year when the delinquency is three years or less and 90 days when it is older. The confirmation order and current statute control each case.

Tennessee Public Chapter 971 / SB 1983 · Tennessee courts delinquent-tax manual
